multiview live test kit  (Apple Silicon)
========================================
Nothing to type or memorize. Three MacBooks, three double-clicks.

  HUB MacBook (also camera 1):  double-click  1-start-hub.command
      It prints a HUB IP.  Leave this window open; it shows [cal] and
      [ext] progress. Ctrl-C in it when you're done.

  SECOND camera MacBook:        double-click  2-start-camera.command
      It asks once for the HUB IP (read it off the hub window), then runs.

  PATTERN MacBook:              double-click  3-show-pattern.command
      A page opens; click it once -> fullscreen, already in calibration
      (mux) mode. Nothing else to press.

Then just give the pattern MOTION where all cameras can see it (shared
overlap): a $15 motorized turntable is ideal (tilt it ~25 deg, walk
away ~2 min), or prop it at a few tilts, or slowly hand-rotate ~60 s.
That is the only manual step, and it is unavoidable: each camera needs
the pattern at several angles (pose diversity) -- a flat screen cannot
fake that in software. All cameras calibrate at once; the shared
display counter yields the camera-to-camera geometry for free.

First double-click may hit macOS Gatekeeper: right-click the .command
-> Open -> Open (once per file). The scripts clear the flag for the
binaries themselves.

Distances note: NO ruler. The 0.1133 built-in is exactly a MacBook Air
13.6"/15"; other Macs are one glance at the pattern page's info (press
i). Pitch only scales reported distances -- intrinsics never need it.
