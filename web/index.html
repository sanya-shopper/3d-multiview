Two cameras watching a molecule

An interactive companion to doc/multiview.pdf: a stylized molecule graph floats in the working volume, observed by the two cameras of a stationary rig. Everything the page computes is a calculation the document derives — pinhole projection (§4), the fundamental matrix and epipolar lines (§6), DLT triangulation (§7), and the depth-error law (§8) — recomputed live as you move the cameras or the molecule.

Drag a camera view to orbit that camera. Click an atom in either view to select it (yellow). The dashed cyan line in each view is the epipolar line of the selected atom's pixel in the other view: the 1D locus where its match must lie.
